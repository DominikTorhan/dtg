How to play

You draw the map for the green ◆ party, which has fewer voters than orange ●. Split the grid into equal districts of connected cells (as many districts as the grid is wide). A district goes to whichever party has a strict majority in it.

Drag across cells to draw a district, or tap cells one by one next to the district you are drawing. Tap a finished district to erase it. Try to win as many districts as the best possible map.

The daily map is the same for everyone and changes at midnight.
