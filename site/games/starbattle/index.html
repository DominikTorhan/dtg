How to play

Place one star in every row, every column and every coloured region. Stars may not touch each other, not even diagonally.

Tap a cell to cycle: empty → dot → star. Dots are your own notes for "no star here". Drag after placing a dot to mark several cells. Right-click toggles a dot. Every puzzle has exactly one solution.

The daily puzzle is the same for everyone and changes at midnight.
