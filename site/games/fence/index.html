How to play

The sheep 🐑 wanders anywhere it can walk (up, down, left, right) and escapes if it can reach the edge of the map. Water stops it.

Tap grass to put down a fence, tap again to take it back. You have a limited number of fences. Fence the sheep in with as much pasture as you can: every enclosed tile is 1 point, and each clover 🍀 inside is worth 3 more.

While the sheep can still escape, red dots show one way out. The daily puzzle is the same for everyone and changes at midnight.
